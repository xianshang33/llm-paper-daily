## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Diagnosing failures in LLM agents is currently largely manual. Practitioners inspect small subsets of execution traces to form ad-hoc hypotheses. This approach misses patterns that only emerge across trace populations and does not scale to production corpora where individual traces can span tens of thousands of tokens.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Current evaluation methods provide aggregate signals based on final task outcomes, failing to reveal intermediate behaviors or localized failure modes within specific input subsets. Many "silent failures" and inefficiencies do not trigger explicit error signals and remain invisible to standard dashboards, requiring population-level analysis for detection.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Insights Generator (IG)**: A multi-agent system designed for corpus-level trace diagnostics. It answers diagnostic questions by proposing and testing hypotheses across the entire trace corpus to produce evidence-backed natural-language insight reports.
- **Challenge 1: Scale and Complexity**. Trace corpora often exceed single-model context windows, and individual traces contain interleaved reasoning, tool calls, and state data. IG addresses this via a "scout-investigator" architecture that systematically explores large corpora through layered processing and hypothesis verification without requiring full context loading.
- **Challenge 2: Credibility and Groundedness of Insights**. Insights must be supported by concrete evidence to avoid hallucinations. IG ensures each natural-language insight is linked to specific supporting trace segments, making the insights "grounded" and enhancing the actionability and trustworthiness of the diagnostic reports.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Evaluation Metrics**: The study evaluates IG on both qualitative and objective dimensions, including rubric-based report assessment and downstream performance improvements resulting from implementing IG insights.
- **Performance Gains**: Human experts using IG reports improved scaffold performance by 30.4 percentage points (pp) over the unmodified baseline. Coding agents leveraging IG-derived insights showed consistent and stable gains.
- **Comparison with Related Work**: Across benchmarks, IG's scout-investigator architecture achieves detection coverage comparable to competing approaches. However, domain experts rated IG reports as superior in terms of depth and evidence quality.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper formalizes the problem of corpus-level trace diagnostics and introduces the Insights Generator (IG). By leveraging multi-agent collaboration, IG automates the extraction of evidence-supported systematic behavioral patterns from large-scale LLM agent execution traces. Experiments demonstrate that IG not only provides high-quality diagnostic reports but also significantly enhances agent performance, effectively addressing deep and silent failures that traditional manual diagnosis and aggregate evaluations miss.
