## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Finance LLM agents must simultaneously block prompt-induced unauthorized actions and approve legitimate multi-step business workflows. Safety is critical as financial operations often involve irreversible state changes (e.g., transfers, KYC leaks).
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Boundary filters are typically stateless and fail to detect fragmented attack payloads spread across multiple turns or hidden in retrieved documents, missing irreversible mid-trajectory tool calls. Post-hoc LLM judges audit only after termination, which is too late for intervention and incurs computational costs scaling linearly with trace length.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed FinHarness**: An inline lifecycle safety harness that wraps a finance agent end-to-end, comprising a Query Monitor, a Tool Monitor, and a Cascade module.
- **Challenge 1: How to detect cross-turn intent drift and fragmented attacks?**
  - **Difficulty**: Individual turns may appear benign, but malicious intent is dispersed across interactions, evading single-point detection.
  - **Solution**: The Query Monitor fuses single-turn intent with cross-turn drift analysis to continuously track cumulative risk in the context.
- **Challenge 2: How to achieve real-time intervention with low latency and cost?**
  - **Difficulty**: Using advanced LLMs for every step is prohibitively expensive, while lightweight models may have high false-negative rates; post-hoc auditing cannot prevent executed errors.
  - **Solution**: The Cascade module integrates per-step risk scores to adaptively route verification between lightweight and advanced LLM judges. Triggered risk factors are re-injected as ex-ante evidence, enabling the agent to autonomously refuse, re-plan, or escalate.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Benchmark**: Evaluated on the FinVault benchmark.
- **Key Results**:
  - **Safety Improvement**: FinHarness reduces the Attack Success Rate (ASR) from 38.3% to 15.0%.
  - **Usability Preservation**: The approval rate for benign requests drops slightly from 41.1% to 39.3%, largely preserving utility.
  - **Efficiency**: Uses 4.7x fewer advanced-judge calls compared to an always-advanced ablation baseline.
- **Comparison**: Outperforms boundary filters by capturing mid-trajectory risks and surpasses post-hoc auditors by enabling in-process intervention at a lower computational cost.

## 4. Conclusion
FinHarness transforms safety protection from "external supervision" to "inline lifecycle management," addressing security challenges in complex multi-step workflows for finance LLM agents. By combining cross-turn intent monitoring with adaptive cascaded verification, it effectively lowers ASR while significantly controlling computational overhead, offering a viable safety framework for high-risk financial agent deployment.
