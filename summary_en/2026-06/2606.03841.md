## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Automating data science workflows, from preprocessing to model evaluation, is a central goal in ML. Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) have enabled autonomous agents capable of reasoning, coding, and coordinating multi-step workflows for end-to-end automation.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current autonomous data science agents (e.g., DS-Agent, AutoKaggle, DeepAnalyze) are fundamentally constrained by static action sets and predefined workflows. They lack principled long-horizon context management, hindering their ability to accumulate reusable experience and operate reliably in iterative, multi-stage pipelines, often failing due to context limits or poor tool selection.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed**: EvoDS, a self-evolving autonomous data science agent with skill learning and adaptive context management.
- **Challenge 1: Static action spaces limit adaptability and experience accumulation.**
  - **Difficulty**: Hard-coded toolsets cannot dynamically generate or optimize operations for novel tasks, leading to poor generalization.
  - **Solution**: Introduces Autonomous Skill Acquisition (ASA), enabling the agent to synthesize, validate, and reuse executable skills via agentic reinforcement learning, thereby dynamically expanding its capabilities.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of principled long-horizon context management leads to information loss or token overflow.**
  - **Difficulty**: Passive truncation strategies disrupt critical historical reasoning chains, affecting coherence in long-term tasks.
  - **Solution**: Proposes Adaptive Context Compression (ACC), treating context management as a learned control problem aligned with the information bottleneck principle, ensuring efficient retention of key information.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Training**: EvoDS orchestrates ASA and ACC within a two-stage multi-agent training scheme, allowing for autonomous improvement over time. Theoretical proofs demonstrate that its hierarchical design reduces tool-selection errors and optimizes context usage.
- **Evaluation Results**: EvoDS outperforms state-of-the-art open-source data science agents by an average of 28.9% across four diverse benchmarks.
- **Comparison**: It significantly improves accuracy and completely eliminates out-of-token failures, demonstrating superior robustness in long-horizon complex tasks compared to existing methods.

## 4. Conclusion
EvoDS addresses the fundamental limitations of static action spaces and passive context management in existing data science agents through Autonomous Skill Acquisition and Adaptive Context Compression. Driven by agentic reinforcement learning, EvoDS achieves significant performance gains across multiple benchmarks, offering a new paradigm for building flexible, reliable, and long-memory capable automated data science systems.
