## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Large Language Models (LLMs) face a fundamental tension between finite context windows and the unbounded context demands of complex, long-horizon real-world tasks. Passive context management methods (e.g., summarization or truncation) lack proactive planning.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: While the paradigm of a main agent delegating subtasks to subagents saves context, it requires "Delegation Intelligence"—the ability to decompose tasks, decide when/how to delegate, and integrate results. Such training data is scarce in natural text, and methods for synthesizing this data and training models remain largely unexplored in the open-source community.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed SearchSwarm Framework and SearchSwarm-30B-A3B Model**: Targeting deep research as a representative long-horizon task, the paper designs a harness to synthesize high-quality training data, internalizing delegation intelligence into model weights via supervised fine-tuning.
- **Challenge 1: Scarcity of High-Quality Delegation Training Data**. The difficulty lies in the rarity of correct task decomposition and delegation decisions in natural text. The paper addresses this by designing a harness that guides the model toward high-quality decomposition and constrains sub-agents, automatically generating trajectories with correct delegation decisions for SFT.
- **Challenge 2: Integration of Sub-agent Results into Main Workflow**. The difficulty is that improper or redundant returns from sub-agents can consume context or break logic. The paper solves this by strictly constraining sub-agents within the harness to return only properly summarized results that support the main agent's workflow.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: The authors release the harness, model weights (SearchSwarm-30B-A3B), and training data. The model is based on a 30B parameter scale (A3B activated).
- **Evaluation Results**:
    - Achieved a score of 68.1 on BrowseComp.
    - Achieved a score of 73.3 on BrowseComp-ZH.
- **Comparison with Related Work**:
    - SearchSwarm achieves the best results among all models of comparable scale.
    - It remains highly competitive against closed-source/open-source models that are over 10× larger (e.g., GPT-5, Claude Opus, Gemini 3), outperforming them in certain Chinese benchmarks.

## 4. Conclusion
SearchSwarm bridges the gap in synthesizing training data and training models for "Delegation Intelligence" in long-horizon agentic tasks. By leveraging a harness-guided approach to generate high-quality SFT data, it enables a 30B-scale model to achieve performance surpassing same-scale models and rivaling much larger models, offering a new paradigm for efficient long-range reasoning under resource constraints.
