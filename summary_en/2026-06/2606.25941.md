## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: The demand for high performance and adaptability in modern control systems has led to increasingly sophisticated controllers, including rigorous nonlinear control and data-driven "black-box" machine learning control. This complexity makes it difficult for engineers and operators to interpret internal decision-making processes.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Traditional model-based controls are mathematically complex and hard to interpret, while data-driven deep learning controls are opaque black boxes. Existing Explainable AI (XAI) methods often lack generality for closed-loop control systems, failing to provide comprehensive, hierarchical, and human-understandable explanations across different model types.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed**: An Explainable Control Framework (XCF) comprising supporting algorithms and a user interface to provide model-agnostic explanations for controllers in closed-loop systems.
- **Challenge 1**: How to accurately capture and explain the behavior logic of complex controllers without relying on their internal structure?
  - **Difficulty & Solution**: Direct analysis is difficult due to black-box nature or mathematical complexity. The paper proposes Hierarchical Fuzzy Model-Agnostic Explanation for Control systems (HFMAE-C), which uses fuzzy logic systems to approximate controller behavior and dynamics, revealing decision logic via IF-THEN rules and providing multi-level explanations (sample to universe).
- **Challenge 2**: How to translate technical explanations into natural language understandable by non-experts and provide interactive consultation?
  - **Difficulty & Solution**: Traditional outputs are numerical or rule-based lists lacking semantic coherence. The paper develops a Large Language Model (LLM) agent-supported UI that automatically analyzes user requirements, selects algorithms, interprets explanations into natural language reports, and offers interactive Q&A.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Case Studies**: Case studies were conducted on an inverted pendulum system and Turtlebot obstacle avoidance. The framework integrates the HFMAE-C algorithm backend with an LLM agent frontend.
- **Evaluation Results**: Simulated user experiments and quantitative comparisons demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed method. HFMAE-C provides sample, local, domain, and universe-level explanations, quantifying state contributions via salience values.
- **Comparison**: Compared to mainstream explainable control approaches, XCF offers model-agnostic explanations and enhances human-machine interaction through LLM integration, providing deeper insights into how controllers determine actions and their underlying mechanisms, thus showing superior understandability and practicality.

#### Summary
This paper presents an Explainable Control Framework (XCF) based on fuzzy model-agnostic explanation and an LLM agent-supported interface. By introducing the HFMAE-C method, it approximates controller behavior using fuzzy logic systems to generate hierarchical IF-THEN rules and salience values, addressing the explainability issues of black-box controllers and complex mathematical models. Furthermore, the integration of an LLM agent enables natural language reporting and interactive consultation, significantly improving user understanding and trust. Experiments on inverted pendulum and robot obstacle avoidance tasks validate the framework's effectiveness and superiority.
