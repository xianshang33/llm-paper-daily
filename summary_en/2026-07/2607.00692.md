## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Long-horizon LLM agents accumulate structured tool results, files, plans, and user constraints. As interactions grow, this context becomes a critical runtime resource affecting cost, latency, and task quality.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Current systems rely on in-run heuristics (e.g., chronological pruning, masking) or final self-summarization. Heuristics are cheap but blind to future dependencies, often deleting critical data. Summaries preserve narrative state but compress exact evidence, locators, and editable artifacts into unaddressable prose, making them unusable for precise downstream tasks.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Self-GC (Self-Governing Context)**: A framework that shifts context management from simple token reclamation to governing the lifecycle of agent context objects.
- **Challenge 1: How to identify and preserve structured evidence critical for future tasks?**
  - **Difficulty**: Traditional position-based or time-based pruning cannot determine if an old tool output contains unique URLs, table values, or file paths needed later.
  - **Solution**: Self-GC converts user turns, tool spans, and skill states into indexed objects. A side-channel planner analyzes dependencies to propose fold, mask, and prune actions, ensuring critical evidence is not blindly discarded.
- **Challenge 2: How to ensure recoverability and safety while aggressively compressing context?**
  - **Difficulty**: Aggressive compression can lead to irreversible information loss, hindering auditing and correct subsequent operations.
  - **Solution**: The system enforces recoverable sidecars, safe commit boundaries, and cache-aware commits. Even if objects are masked or pruned, original content can be restored via sidecars, achieving controlled lifecycle management akin to garbage collection.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Setup**: Evaluated on a 33-session Hard Set and a 332-session production-derived suite.
- **Comparison Results**:
  - On the Hard Set, Self-GC pruned 43.95% of prefix tokens while leaving 84.85% of future continuations unaffected. Baseline heuristics achieved no-impact rates of only 54.55% to 69.70%.
  - On the production suite, Self-GC with three planner backbones reached no-impact rates of 91.27% to 94.58%, whereas baselines remained at 77.71% to 87.46%.
- **Production Deployment**: Online A/B testing showed a 10% to 15% reduction in average daytime input tokens, with peak reductions near 20%.

## 4. Conclusion
Self-GC demonstrates that context management for long-horizon agents should be treated as runtime lifecycle control over indexed, recoverable objects rather than post-hoc text cleanup. By viewing context as a collection of objects with varying lifecycle needs and using a side-channel planner for intelligent governance, Self-GC significantly reduces token consumption while effectively preserving structured information critical for future tasks, outperforming traditional heuristic pruning and summarization methods.
