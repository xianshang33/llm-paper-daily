## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Healthcare appointment scheduling remains a persistent operational bottleneck due to manual coordination, fragmented legacy systems, and high administrative overhead, which constrains provider availability and degrades patient access.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Most existing LLM systems are designed for clinical decision support or information retrieval, lacking the strict safety, reliability, and transactional correctness required for operational healthcare workflows. General-purpose LLMs cannot be directly deployed in real-world healthcare operations due to challenges in safety scope control, precise orchestration of stateful transactions, and the integration of structured database actions with unstructured responses.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed CareConnect**: A safety-first conversational agent for healthcare logistics automation that leverages LLM function calling, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and layered deterministic safety guardrails.
- **Challenge 1: Safety and Scope Control**. Agents must strictly avoid providing medical advice, diagnosis, or mishandling emergencies, which is difficult to guarantee with probabilistic models. The paper addresses this by implementing deterministic pre-LLM intent filtering, where a rule-based classifier intercepts safety-critical requests before LLM invocation, handling them via predefined responses and escalation paths.
- **Challenge 2: Precise Orchestration of Stateful Transactions**. Scheduling involves complex stateful operations (booking, modification, cancellation) where errors cause harm or inconsistency, and current RAG systems focus on knowledge rather than operational logic. The paper solves this using a schema-constrained tool orchestration framework that coordinates eight domain-specific tools with stateful validation to ensure accurate execution of structured database actions.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: CareConnect integrates deterministic intent filtering, scope-aware prompting, and stateful tool validation. The source code is publicly available on GitHub.
- **Evaluation Results**: Evaluated on a comprehensive benchmark of 680 task-oriented scenarios, CareConnect achieved a 91.8% task completion rate with a median latency of 2.2 seconds per request. It demonstrated 96.0% safety compliance on safety-critical subsets and an average operational cost of $0.0324 per appointment.
- **Comparison**: Compared to manual human scheduling, the system yields significant cost reductions while maintaining high safety standards and reliability, proving that carefully scoped LLM agents can automate complex healthcare workflows effectively.

## 4. Conclusion
CareConnect demonstrates that LLM-based agents can be safely deployed in healthcare logistics by employing layered deterministic safety guardrails and strict scope constraints. It successfully addresses the safety and transactional consistency challenges inherent in general LLMs, achieving high task completion rates and low costs, thus providing a viable paradigm for trustworthy AI agents in healthcare operations.
