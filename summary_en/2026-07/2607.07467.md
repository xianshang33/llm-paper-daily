## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Spatial and single-cell transcriptomics are transformative for deciphering cellular dynamics, with Trajectory Inference (TI) being critical for reconstructing cell developmental paths.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Current methods require extensive manual intervention and proficiency in heterogeneous bioinformatics tools, creating significant barriers to efficient and standardized TI analysis.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed**: SpaCellAgent, an autonomous LLM-based multi-agent framework that automates end-to-end spatiotemporal analysis and narrative generation.
- **Challenge 1: Strategic Workflow Planning and Automation**. The difficulty lies in translating vague natural language requirements into precise, executable bioinformatics steps. The paper addresses this by utilizing a multi-agent architecture for strategic workflow planning, which interprets user intent and generates standardized analysis plans, thereby lowering the barrier to entry.
- **Challenge 2: Adaptive Tool Selection and Iterative Performance Optimization**. The challenge is dynamically selecting the most suitable algorithms for diverse sequencing platforms and data structures while maintaining accuracy. The paper introduces a dynamic tool-orchestration engine for adaptive algorithm selection and a self-evolution module that iteratively refines performance through feedback, ensuring expert-aligned quality alongside automation.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Experimental Setup**: Evaluated on six heterogeneous datasets encompassing complex temporal developmental trajectories, diverse sequencing platforms, and spatially-resolved tissue architectures.
- **Results vs. Related Work**: SpaCellAgent demonstrated over a 40% improvement in analytical efficiency while maintaining performance aligned with expert manual analysis. Compared to traditional workflows requiring heavy manual effort, it successfully converts natural language specifications into optimized workflows, achieving full pipeline automation.
- **Advantages**: It not only enhances efficiency but also democratizes advanced spatiotemporal modeling by generating analytical narratives, establishing a scalable, agent-driven paradigm for computational biology.

## 4. Conclusion
SpaCellAgent effectively addresses the high labor costs and complexity of traditional trajectory inference by integrating LLM-based multi-agent collaboration, dynamic tool orchestration, and self-evolution mechanisms. It proves its efficiency and accuracy across multiple heterogeneous datasets, offering an automated, low-barrier, and high-performance solution that significantly advances the accessibility and standardization of spatiotemporal transcriptomic analysis.
