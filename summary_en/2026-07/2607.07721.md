## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Current Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and agentic frameworks are fundamentally reactive, acting only upon human query. This leads to significant productivity costs as knowledge workers spend excessive time searching for existing information and miss time-sensitive signals like expiring contracts or emerging incident dependencies.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Traditional graph-based knowledge representations are typically static and query-driven. They lack a live, delta-aware relational structure capable of continuously tracking state changes, identifying threshold-crossing events, and determining the appropriate personnel for action without explicit prompts.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed a Context Graph**: A dynamic relational data structure that models enterprise entities, their relationships, and state transitions over time, serving as the substrate for proactive agent behavior.
- **Challenge 1: How to accurately capture actionable signals from massive real-time changes?**
  - **Difficulty**: Enterprise data streams are vast and noisy, making it hard to distinguish routine updates from critical threshold-crossing events requiring intervention.
  - **Solution**: Designed a "Delta Detection Engine" that continuously monitors graph state changes to specifically identify events where predefined action thresholds are crossed.
- **Challenge 2: How to ensure pushed information is highly relevant and urgent for specific individuals?**
  - **Difficulty**: Simple alerts generate noise; if they do not match the recipient's role and urgency needs, they lead to user fatigue or ignorance.
  - **Solution**: Derived a unified "Proactivity Score" function that ranks candidate insights based on urgency, relevance, persona-fit, and confidence, using an LLM-powered surfacing layer to deliver grounded, explanatory notifications.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: Provides a complete end-to-end Python implementation using NetworkX for graph management and the Anthropic Claude API for natural language notification generation. The system includes schema definition, delta detection, scoring, and notification layers.
- **Evaluation Results**: Evaluated across three generic enterprise case studies: contract lifecycle management, engineering incident response, and sales pipeline hygiene.
- **Comparison & Performance**:
  - **Precision@5**: Achieved 0.83, indicating high relevance in top recommendations.
  - **False Positive Rate**: Low at 0.11, demonstrating minimal noise.
  - **Efficiency Gain**: Reduced the mean time to surface information from 47 minutes (reactive baseline) to under 30 seconds, significantly outperforming traditional passive query modes.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper addresses the architectural limitation of reactive enterprise AI agents by proposing a proactive information surfacing system based on the "Context Graph." By constructing a dynamic graph structure, a delta detection engine, and a multi-dimensional proactivity scoring mechanism, the system proactively pushes high-value, high-urgency insights before users ask. Experiments demonstrate that this method significantly reduces the time for critical information to reach users while maintaining a low false positive rate, offering a new technical path for enhancing enterprise productivity.
