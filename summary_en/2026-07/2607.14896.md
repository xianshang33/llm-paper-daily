## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Addressing structural engineering requests requires a chain of interdependent artifacts, including interpreted requirements, computable models, validation records, solver outputs, code-check records, and final reports, rather than a single answer.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current evaluations focused on question answering or script generation rarely verify this complete evidence chain. Consequently, they may reward fluent outputs even when the underlying engineering workflow is incomplete, internally inconsistent, or non-executable.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed StructureClaw**: An artifact-centered workbench where LLM agents operate via governed engineering skills, typed tools, shared artifact state, and local analysis backends. It also introduces StructureClaw-Bench, an executable benchmark with 150 controlled scenarios.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in verifying workflow integrity and executability**. Traditional text-based evaluations fail to detect failures caused by missing or inconsistent upstream artifacts. StructureClaw addresses this by defining success only when all required artifact- and execution-level assertions pass in a single run, ensuring workflow completeness.
- **Challenge 2: Insufficient robustness in complex interactions and multimodal reconstruction**. Handling invalid numerical inputs safely and reconstructing structural models with fixture consistency remains difficult. The paper exposes these workflow-level failures through interactive and multimodal evaluations, providing specific directions for improvement.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: StructureClaw integrates governed engineering skills, typed tools, and local analysis backends (e.g., OpenSeesPy) to support a traceable workflow from requirements to reports. Code and benchmarks are open-sourced.
- **Evaluation Results**: Across ten agent-model configurations evaluated on 50 standard cases, the average Success Rate increased from 56.8% with a generic-skill baseline to 88.6% with the full automatic workflow provided by StructureClaw.
- **Comparison and Findings**: Unlike traditional evaluations focusing solely on final text, StructureClaw-Bench reveals workflow-level failures. Interactive and multimodal assessments identified two prominent remaining challenges: safe handling of invalid numerical inputs and fixture-consistent reconstruction of structural models.

## 4. Conclusion
StructureClaw addresses the lack of complete evidence chain verification in evaluating structural engineering LLM agents by introducing an artifact-centered evaluation paradigm. Its executable benchmark, StructureClaw-Bench, rigorously assesses agent capabilities in workflow execution, interactive robustness, and multimodal reconstruction, significantly enhancing evaluation strictness and providing a traceable, executable foundation for future agent development.
