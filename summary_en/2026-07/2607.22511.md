## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Automating theoretical research is bottlenecked by both the generation of candidate results and their reliable evaluation. In theoretical domains, incorrect theorems are hard to distinguish from correct ones without expert verification, which is slow and expensive.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Current systems often rely on Large Language Models (LLMs) as reviewers to close the research loop. However, empirical studies show LLM reviewers are unreliable, accepting fabricated papers at high rates while detecting fabrications at near-chance levels, making them unsuitable for ensuring correctness.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed CausalForge**: A formally grounded, self-improving agentic framework for automated theoretical research in causal inference, built upon the Lean proof assistant.
- **Challenge 1: High Cost and Lack of Foundations for Formalization**. Formalizing results from scratch is labor-intensive and lacks reusable infrastructure for causal inference. **Solution**: Developed Causalean, a foundational Lean library with 7,035 machine-checked declarations (identification theorems, estimators, etc.), allowing agents to build proofs on verified infrastructure rather than starting from zero.
- **Challenge 2: Faithfulness Gap between Formal Statements and Scientific Intent**. A proof passing kernel verification may still fail to capture the intended scientific claim (e.g., by introducing axioms or altering hypotheses). **Solution**: Integrated a "statement audit" mechanism in the CausalSmith agent pipeline that compares each formal theorem against its intended informal claim, ensuring semantic fidelity beyond mere syntactic correctness.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: CausalForge combines Causalean (the library) and CausalSmith (the self-improving agent pipeline). CausalSmith handles topic selection, result proposal, formalization, proof construction, and artifact presentation. The source code, library, and run records are open-sourced on GitHub.
- **Evaluation Results**: Evaluated using artifacts from completed autonomous research runs, demonstrating the system's ability to generate machine-checked proofs that are semantically aligned with scientific claims.
- **Comparison**: Unlike LLM-reviewer-based systems (e.g., AI-Scientist) which suffer from high false acceptance rates for fabricated content, CausalForge leverages Lean's kernel for absolute mathematical correctness and adds statement auditing to address semantic gaps, offering a significantly more reliable framework for automated research.

#### Summary
CausalForge addresses the reliability crisis in automated theoretical research by grounding causal inference in rigorous Lean formalization. By providing a large-scale reusable formal library and introducing a novel statement audit mechanism to bridge the gap between formal proofs and scientific intent, it establishes a new paradigm for high-trust automated scientific discovery.
