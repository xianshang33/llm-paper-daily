## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: As LLM agents evolve, they rely on expanding external tool libraries scaling to thousands of APIs (e.g., ToolBench with 16,464 endpoints). Due to context window limits and degraded information utility in long contexts, injecting all tools is impractical, necessitating the retrieval of small, relevant tool subsets.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Current retrievers either score tools independently (semantic matching like BM25/Contriever) or assemble sets sequentially. These paradigms fail to evaluate the joint utility of a candidate set as a whole, ignoring synergistic effects where individually low-scoring tools may be critical in combination, or high-scoring tools may collectively fail to cover the task.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed HYSET (HYperedge-based SEt-level Tool retrieval)**: A framework that formulates tool retrieval as query-conditioned hyperedge prediction on a tool co-invocation hypergraph.
- **Challenge 1: How to evaluate the joint utility of a tool set rather than individual scores?**
  - **Difficulty**: Traditional methods treat tools as isolated islands, missing the collaborative capability of a group to solve a task.
  - **Solution**: The paper constructs a tool co-invocation hypergraph and treats the tool set itself as the scoring unit (hyperedge). By performing query-conditioned hyperedge prediction, it directly scores the overall relevance of candidate sets, naturally capturing joint utility.
- **Challenge 2: How to handle tool compatibility and interactions for varying set sizes?**
  - **Difficulty**: Tool combinations of different cardinalities have distinct interaction patterns and compatibility requirements, which fixed-dimensional representations struggle to adapt to.
  - **Solution**: Introduces cardinality-specific interactions to explicitly capture size-dependent tool compatibility, allowing the model to accurately assess the validity of tool sets of varying scales.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: HYSET is designed as a pre-selection module that requires no modifications to the downstream agent, offering plug-and-play usability.
- **Evaluation Results**:
  - Experiments on ToolBench show that HYSET consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines in both tool retrieval metrics and end-to-end task success rates.
  - **Generalization**: Beyond in-domain performance, HYSET supports zero-shot/few-shot transfer, generalizing to held-out tools, new categories, and unseen domains with minimal supervision.
- **Comparison**: Compared to traditional semantic matching (e.g., Contriever) and sequential assembly methods, HYSET significantly improves retrieval quality by addressing the lack of synergistic effect modeling through set-level scoring.

#### Summary
This paper addresses the bottleneck of large-scale tool retrieval for LLM agents by highlighting the limitations of independent scoring paradigms. By proposing HYSET, it reformulates retrieval as query-conditioned hyperedge prediction and introduces cardinality-specific interactions to capture tool compatibility. The method serves as an easy-to-deploy pre-selection module without altering the agent, surpassing SOTA baselines in retrieval accuracy and end-to-end success, while demonstrating robust cross-domain and zero-shot generalization capabilities.
