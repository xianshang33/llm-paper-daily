## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Financial indicators are crucial tools that transform raw financial data into interpretable measures for valuation, risk assessment, and economic analysis. Constructing these indicators involves complex steps including formula definition, data collection, numerical calculation, and answer generation.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Current financial benchmarks primarily focus on final answer accuracy and often assume that relevant data is already provided. This approach overlooks the evaluation of the intermediate processes in indicator construction, failing to comprehensively assess an agent's end-to-end capabilities in real-world financial analysis scenarios.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed FinDeepIndicator**: The first benchmark dedicated to evaluating Deep Research (DR) agents in end-to-end financial indicator construction.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in Fine-grained Evaluation of Multi-stage Processes**. Financial indicator construction involves four tightly coupled stages: formula specification, data collection, calculation, and answer generation. Traditional benchmarks struggle to isolate errors in each step. FinDeepIndicator addresses this by designing an evaluation framework covering these four stages across 21 sub-categories of fundamental, technical, and macroeconomic indicators, enabling precise diagnosis of reasoning and execution capabilities at each step.
- **Challenge 2: Difficulty in Acquiring and Verifying Real-world Cross-market Data**. Realistic financial analysis requires handling massive, heterogeneous, and noisy historical data from different markets (e.g., US and China). The benchmark includes 10 years of historical financial data for 800 listed companies and 3,350 curated QA pairs, providing a high-standard testbed for assessing data retrieval and numerical execution reliability in complex environments.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Experimental Setup**: Extensive experiments were conducted on search-equipped Large Language Models (LLMs) and specialized Deep Research (DR) agents.
- **Evaluation Results**:
    - **LLM Performance**: Search-equipped LLMs perform well in "formula specification" but suffer a substantial drop in accuracy during "data retrieval" and "numerical execution," highlighting their limitations in handling specific financial data and complex calculations.
    - **DR Agent Performance**: DR agents consistently outperform search-equipped LLMs across all stages, demonstrating superior multi-step reasoning and tool-use capabilities.
    - **Overall Conclusion**: Despite being better, DR agents remain unreliable in realistic financial analysis settings, particularly showing significant defects in data accuracy and computational rigor.
- **Comparison**: Unlike traditional benchmarks that only evaluate final answers, FinDeepIndicator reveals weaknesses in intermediate processes, identifying the main bottlenecks for deploying current technologies in finance.

#### Summary
This paper introduces FinDeepIndicator, filling the gap in end-to-end financial indicator construction evaluation. Through fine-grained multi-stage assessment and real-world data covering US and Chinese markets, the study finds that while Deep Research agents surpass standard search-augmented LLMs, their instability in data retrieval and numerical calculation remains a major barrier to trustworthy application in finance. This work provides critical insights and a benchmark for developing more capable and reliable financial DR agents.
