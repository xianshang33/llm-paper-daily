## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Spreadsheets are widely used for organizing semi-structured data, but automated reasoning remains challenging for Large Language Models (LLMs). Real-world workbooks often contain implicit cross-table associations, fine-grained column dependencies, and complex spatial layouts.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current methods typically "flatten" multi-dimensional spreadsheet structures into sequential strings (e.g., Markdown or JSON). This process strips away critical intra-sheet boundaries and inter-sheet semantics, depriving LLMs of the global spatial context that human experts naturally leverage, leading to poor performance on complex tasks.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed SheetCompass**, a graph-guided and memory-driven agentic framework for spreadsheet reasoning and automation. It transforms raw tabular data into structured evidence spaces to guide agent planning, execution, and reflection.
- **Challenge 1: Loss of Spatial Structure and Implicit Semantics**. Spreadsheets have multi-dimensional structures and implicit dependencies; traditional linearization destroys these topological relationships, making global layout understanding difficult. **Solution**: SheetCompass constructs a Hierarchical Relation Graph that formalizes implicit layouts into stable anchors at both table and column levels, explicitly encoding spatial relations and preserving structural topology.
- **Challenge 2: Error Accumulation and Lack of Domain Knowledge in Long-horizon Tasks**. Agents in complex workflows are prone to repetitive errors and lack specific tool-use knowledge. **Solution**: Introduces a dual-level memory mechanism: "Expert Knowledge Memory" for domain/tool knowledge, and "Reasoning Experience Memory" dynamically updated from past trajectories to prevent continuous mistakes. It also coordinates three specialized agents (Navigational Explorer, Logical Programmer, Critical Reflector) for perception, execution, and iterative verification.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: SheetCompass employs a multi-agent architecture comprising a Navigational Explorer (perception/localization), Logical Programmer (code generation/execution), and Critical Reflector (verification/correction). The system integrates the hierarchical graph structure with the dual-memory module. Code is available online.
- **Evaluation Results**: Extensive empirical evaluations across various datasets demonstrate that SheetCompass achieves state-of-the-art (SOTA) performance in complex spreadsheet automation tasks.
- **Comparison**: Compared to existing methods relying on information flattening, SheetCompass significantly improves reasoning accuracy and robustness by preserving spatial structures and leveraging memory mechanisms, especially excelling in workbooks with complex cross-table associations and fine-grained dependencies.

#### Summary
SheetCompass addresses the key issue of lost spatial semantics in LLM-based spreadsheet processing caused by structure flattening. By introducing a Hierarchical Relation Graph to explicitly model intra- and inter-sheet structural relationships, and combining it with a dual-memory mechanism and multi-agent collaboration, the method effectively enhances the agent's ability to plan, execute, and self-correct in complex workflows, achieving significant improvements in automated spreadsheet reasoning performance.
