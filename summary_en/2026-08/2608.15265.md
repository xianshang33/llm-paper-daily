## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Constructing interactive 3D open worlds from user queries is crucial for gaming, simulation, and embodied AI. The rise of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) has spurred interest in automating this process via agents.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Current methods are primarily evaluated on idealized, simple queries, making it difficult to systematically analyze how agents understand intent, use 3D tools, and reason over multimodal data. Furthermore, the lack of open-source frameworks hinders research into whether training (e.g., agentic RL post-training) can improve these capabilities.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed VibeWorlding Framework**: A unified framework for benchmarking and training "vibe worlding" agents that can autonomously infer intent, plan layouts, invoke 3D tools, and reflect on multimodal feedback in multi-turn interactions.
- **Challenge 1: Lack of high-quality, diverse evaluation benchmarks**. Existing benchmarks are too simple to reflect real-world complexity. **Solution**: Built VWE-BENCH, comprising 2,616 high-quality 3D assets, 323 human-annotated seed worlds, and 6,828 reverse-synthesized multimodal queries, split into verified (with ground truth) and unverified (with rubrics) sets.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of unified environments and reward mechanisms for RL training**. It is difficult to optimize multi-step decision-making via RL in existing setups. **Solution**: Developed VibeWorlding-Gym, a joint multimodal RL post-training framework integrating a sandbox environment (unifying asset retrieval, editing, rendering) and a rubric-based verifier (checking physical feasibility and intent fulfillment), enabling fair evaluation and scalable RL rewards.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Results**: Current frontier MLLMs struggle with the task, with even GPT-5.5 and Qwen3.8-Max achieving below 60% success rate. The primary bottleneck is precise 3D world editing.
- **Impact of RL Training**: RL post-training alleviates this weakness, allowing open-source MLLMs to surpass closed-source frontiers.
- **Model Comparison**:
    - VibeWorlder-8B is comparable to frontier MLLMs.
    - The flagship VibeWorlder-30B-A3B achieves the best overall Pass@1 among all evaluated models.
- **Comparison with Related Work**: Unlike sequential pipeline methods (e.g., SceneCraft, 3D-GPT) or prompt-only interaction methods (e.g., SAGE, SceneWeaver), VibeWorlding significantly improves intent understanding and tool usage in complex scenarios through RL post-training.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper introduces the VibeWorlding framework to address the limitations in evaluation standards and training mechanisms for 3D world construction agents. By constructing the large-scale VWE-BENCH benchmark and the RL-supportive VibeWorlding-Gym environment, it demonstrates that RL post-training significantly enhances the performance of open-source MLLMs in complex 3D scene construction, enabling them to match or exceed state-of-the-art closed-source models.
