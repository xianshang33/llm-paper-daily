## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: AI-powered digital avatar streamers in live e-commerce must answer product questions, interact with viewers, and execute marketing strategies in real-time. This requires ultra-low latency, high adaptability to frequent changes in campaign rules and compliance, and accurate, effective responses.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Traditional ReAct-style agents struggle with agile strategy updates, often requiring full redeployment or retraining, which is too slow for live commerce. Large general-purpose models have strong generalization but excessive latency for real-time use. Conversely, compact models fine-tuned on fixed configurations overfit to specific prompts and schemas, failing to adapt when the execution environment (Harness) changes.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed an Evolvable Harness Architecture and Harness-Aware Training (HAT)**: The architecture decouples Skills, Hooks, system prompts, and tools from model weights, allowing runtime behavior changes without retraining. HAT trains compact models to adapt to this dynamic environment.
- **Challenge 1: Overfitting and Memorization in Compact Models**. The difficulty lies in compact models memorizing names, schemas, and templates of a fixed configuration rather than understanding the current Harness state, leading to failure upon environmental changes. Solution: Introduced Harness-State Augmentation (HSA), which applies task-preserving transformations to skill identifiers, content, tool schemas, and prompt structures during training, teaching the model to understand the current Harness state instead of memorizing a fixed version.
- **Challenge 2: Maintaining General Instruction Following Under Low Latency Constraints**. The difficulty is that domain-specific or Harness-specific fine-tuning typically degrades general capabilities (e.g., dropping IFEval scores), while strong zero-shot models are too slow. Solution: Employed a three-stage training strategy comprising HSA-based SFT, General Domain On-Policy Distillation (to recover general capabilities), and HSA-based Agentic RL, ensuring the model adapts to Harness changes without sacrificing general instruction following.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: The system runs on a single NVIDIA H20 GPU with MTP optimization. The training pipeline includes SFT, distillation, and RL, evaluated in a production-informed live-room simulator.
- **Evaluation Results**:
    - **Live-Stream QA**: The HAT-trained 35B compact model scored 94.8, significantly outperforming the base model (80.3) and the strongest evaluated general LLM (93.0).
    - **Harness-Variant QA**: Scored 94.6, compared to 75.4 for the base model, demonstrating robust adaptation to environmental changes.
    - **General Capability Retention**: Maintained an IFEval score of 83.5. In contrast, Fixed-Harness SFT caused a 7.7-point drop in IFEval, showing HAT effectively prevents catastrophic forgetting.
    - **Latency**: Achieved a P50 latency of 3.407s and P95 of 8.114s, meeting feasibility requirements for real-time interaction.

#### Summary
This paper addresses the conflict between low latency and high adaptability for live e-commerce digital avatars by proposing an Evolvable Harness architecture and Harness-Aware Training (HAT). By leveraging Harness-State Augmentation and a three-stage training strategy, the authors successfully trained a compact 35B model that adapts to frequent runtime environment changes without weight retraining. Experiments show that this method surpasses the strongest general LLMs in real-time QA accuracy while maintaining strong general instruction-following capabilities and production-feasible low latency, effectively resolving the core tension between compact model overfitting and environmental evolution.
