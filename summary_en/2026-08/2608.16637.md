## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Large Language Models (LLMs) remain unreliable for long-horizon planning, often producing logically inconsistent or inapplicable plans. Hybrid approaches attempt to mitigate this by translating natural language into Planning Domain Definition Language (PDDL), enabling symbolic planners to generate verifiable plans.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current PDDL generation methods often rely on rigid pipelines, partial PDDL definitions, or human feedback. Furthermore, the field lacks standardized benchmarks with automated verification, hindering robust evaluation and reproducibility.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed PDDLCoder**: An agentic framework for iterative PDDL generation from natural language, and **NL-pddlgym**, a new benchmark dataset.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in ensuring accuracy and completeness of PDDL generation**. Complex planning domains make one-shot generation prone to syntax errors and logical flaws, while traditional methods lack self-correction. PDDLCoder addresses this by using an Agent architecture that iteratively reads, edits, and refines PDDL files based on syntax error feedback, significantly reducing failure rates.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of automated and standardized evaluation systems**. Previous studies relied on manual checks or non-standard tests, making objective comparison difficult. The authors constructed NL-pddlgym, comprising 711 problems across 23 domains with executable Gym environments, enabling automated verification of plan applicability and providing a reproducible benchmark.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: PDDLCoder operates as an agent framework that receives natural language descriptions, uses an LLM Agent to create and edit PDDL files via tools, and validates plans within the NL-pddlgym environment.
- **Evaluation Results**: On a test set of 106 problems across 4 held-out domains, PDDLCoder generated applicable plans for 89.6% of the problems.
- **Comparison**:
    - Outperforms adapted previous PDDL generation methods (which achieved up to 45.3%).
    - Outperforms direct LLM planning approaches (which reached up to 74.5%).
    - Demonstrates the superior effectiveness of agentic PDDL generation.

#### Summary
This paper addresses the unreliability of LLMs in long-horizon planning and the lack of evaluation standards in hybrid methods by introducing PDDLCoder and NL-pddlgym. Through iterative generation and automated verification, PDDLCoder significantly improves PDDL accuracy and plan applicability, surpassing baseline methods. The work highlights the advantages of agentic symbolic planning and establishes a crucial standardized benchmark for future research in LLM-assisted planning.
