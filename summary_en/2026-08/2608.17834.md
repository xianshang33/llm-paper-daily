## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are driving data science towards increasingly autonomous agentic workflows, supporting multi-step, long-running analyses. Analysts' roles are shifting from step-by-step drivers to high-level overseers.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current interactive approaches are largely designed for discrete, turn-by-turn exchanges. They fail to capture the parallel branches and evolving decision structures characteristic of long-running agentic analysis. Consequently, they lack adequate support for observability (understanding evolving reasoning) and steerability (redirecting execution during runtime).

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed AdaLens**: An interactive system for monitoring and steering ongoing long-running agentic data analysis runs.
- **Challenge 1: Observability in Long-Running Analysis**. The complex, branching, and iterative nature of agentic analysis makes it difficult to maintain a coherent understanding of the agent's reasoning using linear interfaces. AdaLens addresses this by employing a storyline-based representation that unifies analytical plans, execution progress, intermediate findings, and data-column involvement, providing a holistic view of the analytical process.
- **Challenge 2: Dynamic Steering During Execution**. Analysts need to intervene in real-time to prune unproductive paths or deepen promising ones, but existing tools often only allow pre-execution planning edits. AdaLens enables grounded steering interactions, allowing analysts to provide directional guidance and execution control based on specific analytical elements within the storyline.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Evaluation**: The system was evaluated through two case studies and a user study.
- **Results**: The studies examined how AdaLens supports analysts in monitoring and steering long-running agentic data analysis, demonstrating its effectiveness in enhancing understanding and control.
- **Comparison**: Unlike existing works that focus on discrete interactions or static visualizations, AdaLens specifically targets the needs of long-running, parallel agentic workflows, offering superior observability of branching logic and real-time steerability.

## 4. Conclusion
AdaLens addresses the critical gaps in observability and steerability for long-running agentic data analysis. By leveraging storyline visualization, it structures complex analytical processes and enables context-aware runtime intervention. This work highlights the necessary interface support for the shifting role of analysts from drivers to overseers in human-AI collaborative data science.
