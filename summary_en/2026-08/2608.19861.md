## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Customer-service LLM agents must strictly adhere to organizational policies when acting on behalf of users. Compliance failures typically stem from two sources: executing forbidden actions (e.g., granting ineligible changes) or omitting procedural requirements (e.g., identity verification, confirmation steps).
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current runtime safeguards (e.g., PolicyGuard) only intervene locally at risky actions, failing to guide agents through multi-step procedures. Conversely, workflow-following systems (e.g., SOP-Agent) focus on process completion rather than safeguarding agent behavior, leaving a gap in simultaneous process guidance and security compliance.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed PolicyGuide Framework**: A method that compiles domain policies into workflow graphs and invokes a proactive verifier at user-turn boundaries, shifting from "guarding single actions" to "guiding the whole workflow."
- **Challenge 1: Action-local checks cannot cover multi-step procedural dependencies**. The difficulty lies in compliance requirements spanning multiple dialogue turns (e.g., diagnose-instruct-verify sequences in telecom) without interceptable tool calls in between. PolicyGuide addresses this by persisting graph state and reconciling open requests at each turn, ensuring the agent proceeds along a policy-compliant path.
- **Challenge 2: Existing workflow systems lack behavioral safeguarding**. The challenge is that traditional workflow systems execute prescribed processes but do not dynamically correct agent deviations or resist malicious user inducement. PolicyGuide introduces a proactive verifier that not only guides steps but also gates and remediates agent actions at key nodes, ensuring strict policy adherence.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: PolicyGuide compiles natural language policies into workflow graphs, requiring no model retraining. It was tested across frontier models including GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 2.5 Pro, demonstrating model agnosticism and transferability.
- **Evaluation Results**: Evaluated on the $\tau^2$-bench across airline, retail, and telecom domains. PolicyGuide increased the mean $\\mathrm{Pass}^4$ score from 0.42 to 0.62. The most significant gain was observed in the telecom domain (the most workflow-structured), rising from 0.19 to 0.61.
- **Comparison with Related Work**: Compared to baselines that only intercept actions or workflows that only track completion, PolicyGuide achieved the lowest attack-success rate under adversarial conditions and demonstrated the strongest procedural compliance in author-designed workflow-level validations.

## 4. Conclusion
PolicyGuide innovatively combines external workflow guidance with runtime behavioral monitoring, addressing compliance challenges for LLM agents in complex multi-step tasks. By translating policies into executable workflow graphs and introducing proactive verification, it significantly enhances agent performance in regulated environments, particularly in highly structured domains like telecom, offering a new paradigm for building safe and compliant customer service agents.
