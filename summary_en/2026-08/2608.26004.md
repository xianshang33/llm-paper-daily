## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Agentic LLM pipelines face escalating inference costs as context accumulates from retrieval, tool use, and multi-turn interactions. Deployments often compress inputs to control latency, which degrades task accuracy.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Standard Speculative Decoding (SD) accelerates generation losslessly but assumes the drafter and verifier share identical contexts. This symmetry prevents SD from resolving the accuracy-overhead trade-off: it cannot recover information discarded by compression if the verifier uses a compressed view, nor can it reduce costs if both models use the full context.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed AsymSpec**: An asymmetric speculative decoding framework that breaks the symmetry of context access between the drafter and the verifier.
- **Challenge 1: How to guide generation using full context information when the verifier only sees compressed context?**
  - **Difficulty**: Traditional SD requires identical inputs; directly fusing logits from different contexts causes distribution mismatch and instability.
  - **Solution**: The lightweight drafter reads the full input while the large verifier operates on the compressed view. The drafter computes a contrastive $\\delta$-fusion of logits by subtracting outputs from compressed and full contexts, isolating the information gain provided by the uncompressed input, and fuses this signal into the verifier's logits.
- **Challenge 2: How to ensure verification stability and high acceptance rates when injecting additional information gains?**
  - **Difficulty**: Directly injecting difference signals may deviate the verifier from its original distribution, lowering draft token acceptance rates and negating acceleration benefits.
  - **Solution**: A divergence-aware acceptance gate modulates the fused logits, preserving verification stability and maintaining high draft acceptance rates while retaining critical reasoning signals.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Evaluation Setup**: Evaluated across four agentic capabilities and two end-to-end agent benchmarks.
- **Key Results**:
  - **Accuracy**: AsymSpec achieves approximately 90% of full-context accuracy on average.
  - **Efficiency**: Delivers 1.3–1.7× throughput speedups at 0.2–0.3× the compute cost on isolated text capabilities.
- **Comparison**: Unlike standard SD which is limited to accelerating a fixed target distribution, AsymSpec yields substantial gains precisely when compression discards critical reasoning signals, effectively balancing accuracy and computational overhead.

## 4. Conclusion
AsymSpec innovatively addresses the inference bottleneck in Agent LLMs by decoupling context access for the drafter and verifier. By allowing the drafter to leverage full context to compensate for information loss in the verifier's compressed view, and employing contrastive logit fusion with a divergence-aware gate, it ensures both stability and efficiency. The method significantly reduces computational costs while preserving most task accuracy, offering a new paradigm for efficient long-context Agent deployment.
