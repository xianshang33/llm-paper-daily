## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Ensuring the safety of Autonomous Driving Systems (ADSs) relies heavily on scenario-based testing. However, current pipelines are fragmented, with scenario generation, retrieval, modification, execution, and analysis handled by separate, non-interacting tools requiring significant manual scripting.
- **Limitations of Prior Work**: Existing LLM applications in ADS are confined to specific sub-systems or solely focus on scenario generation. No prior work provides a unified LLM-agent framework covering the entire lifecycle of scenario-based testing, leading to rigid, inefficient, and disjointed validation processes.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed PlannerForge**: An LLM-agent framework that automates all stages of scenario-based testing (from Generation to Assessment) and introduces two new LLM-enhanced stages: ADS Enhancement and ADS Benchmarking.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in End-to-End Automation and Coordination**. Traditional pipelines are siloed, making it hard to maintain user intent across stages. **Solution**: PlannerForge chains modules end-to-end using LLM agents, retaining 83% (commercial) and 78% (open-source) of seed queries, thereby creating a cohesive, automated closed-loop system.
- **Challenge 2: Ensuring Physical Validity and Attribute Adherence in Scenarios**. Generated scenarios often lack physical realism or fail to follow complex natural language constraints. **Solution**: The framework outperforms Scenario Factory 2.0 in executable generation (193 vs. 144 out of 200) and achieves >=94% physically valid edits (vs. 31% for From-Words-to-Collisions), while satisfying 92-96% of requested city, road, and vehicle attributes.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Setup**: Evaluated using 10 off-the-shelf LLMs (including commercial APIs and open-source models like Qwen3.6:35B) across all tasks under 5 prompt conditions.
- **Key Results**:
    - **Performance**: Best per-task scores range from 0.88 to 1.00. Open-source 20-35B backends match commercial APIs on most tasks; Qwen3.6:35B matches commercial APIs on three out of five tasks.
    - **Comparisons**:
        - **Generation**: Outperforms Scenario Factory 2.0 (193 vs. 144 executable scenarios).
        - **Selection**: Outperforms BM25 at Rank 1 selection (92.0% vs. 67.5%).
        - **Modification**: Outperforms From-Words-to-Collisions in physical validity (>=94% vs. 31%).
    - **ADS Enhancement**: Without domain-specific fine-tuning, cost-tuning improved planner success rate from 50.4% to 70.2% and reduced collisions from 19.0% to 8.4% (N=400).

## 4. Conclusion
PlannerForge addresses the gap in unified LLM-agent frameworks for ADS scenario-based testing. It successfully integrates fragmented testing stages into an automated end-to-end system, outperforming SOTA methods (e.g., Scenario Factory 2.0, BM25) in generation, retrieval, and modification metrics. The study demonstrates that mid-sized open-source models can rival commercial APIs in this domain and highlights the framework's ability to significantly enhance planner safety and efficiency through LLM-driven parameter optimization without fine-tuning.
