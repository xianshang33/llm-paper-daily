## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Running LLM agents in production is expensive. Frontier models are accurate but can cost up to 25 times more per token than small models, a gap that compounds in multi-step workflows.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current routing methods (e.g., cascade routing like FrugalGPT) make decisions per call, lacking visibility into the entire workflow structure and subtask dependencies. This leads to error propagation from early steps that downstream models cannot correct. Other methods require separate training data or operate at the agent level rather than the subtask level, failing to leverage dependency information visible during planning.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Planner-as-Router (PaR)**: A joint plan-time model routing framework that integrates model tier selection directly into the planning process.
- **Challenge 1: Lack of Global Dependency Awareness**. Traditional per-call routers view nodes in isolation, missing inter-subtask dependencies and leading to compounding errors. **Solution**: PaR assigns model tiers (small, mid, frontier) to subtasks as the planner decomposes the query, making dependencies visible before execution and enabling globally optimized routing.
- **Challenge 2: Overhead and Data Requirements**. High-performance routers often need separate models or labeled training data. **Solution**: PaR requires no separate router model or training data, leveraging the planning process itself for routing decisions without additional training or inference overhead.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Benchmark**: Evaluated using EntBench, comprising 54 enterprise agentic tasks across seven classes, graded by executing generated SQL and MongoDB queries against live databases.
- **Scale**: Over 1,157 evaluations spanning eight routers and three seeds.
- **Results**:
    - PaR remains on the observed cost-accuracy frontier.
    - It matches the accuracy of a "sink-frontier" heuristic (frontier model only on terminal nodes) at comparable cost.
    - It outperforms a faithful FrugalGPT cascade at a lower cost.
    - Compared to all-frontier routing, PaR reduces costs by 44% with only a 2.9-point drop in accuracy.
    - The accuracy difference falls within the confidence interval, framing PaR's advantage as achieving a superior position on the cost-accuracy frontier.
- **Open Source**: PaR, EntBench, and all evaluation code are open source.

## 4. Conclusion
Planner-as-Router (PaR) addresses the high cost of multi-agent workflows by integrating model routing into the planning phase. By assigning model tiers based on global workflow dependencies, it avoids the error propagation issues of per-call routing and eliminates the need for extra training data. Experiments demonstrate that PaR significantly reduces costs while maintaining competitive accuracy, offering an efficient solution for enterprise LLM deployments.
