## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Equity-relevant news evolves through temporally dependent corporate events rather than isolated observations. Effective financial reasoning requires not only current evidence but also a temporally valid representation of how this evidence relates to historical event dynamics.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Existing LLM-based financial agents incorporate historical evidence but offer limited support for preserving issuer-specific chronology under point-in-time constraints and fail to identify when historical transitions contribute information beyond the current forecast.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed RICE-Alpha**: A point-in-time stock-scoring framework that separates a history-aware multi-view Base Alpha from a reliability-calibrated residual correction derived from historical event continuation.
- **Challenge 1: How to maintain issuer-specific historical continuity under strict point-in-time constraints?**
  - **Difficulty**: Directly retrieving historical news risks introducing look-ahead bias or confusing timelines across different issuers, leading to data leakage or logical errors.
  - **Solution**: Introduces a "Multi-Tier Memory Layer" that grounds news interpretation in temporally eligible, issuer-specific history, ensuring only historically available and relevant information is considered.
- **Challenge 2: How to quantify the actual contribution of historical event transitions and avoid noise?**
  - **Difficulty**: Not all historical event continuations have predictive value; blind integration introduces noise, and measuring the reliability of specific event paths is difficult.
  - **Solution**: Uses a "Typed Event Agent" to construct event states, forming successor relations within issuers and pooling across firms only after valid local pairing. It calibrates matured transitions by empirical reliability and adds the resulting graph signal as a residual (RICE Delta) to the base forecast.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Experimental Setup**: Tested on daily Nasdaq-100 and Hang Seng Index panels from 2024 to 2026.
- **Comparison Results**: RICE-Alpha achieved the strongest results among all evaluated LLM-based agents and momentum strategies, outperforming baselines across four predictive and four portfolio-level metrics.
- **Specific Performance**: Its ICIR more than doubled that of the strongest baseline. Net Sharpe ratios reached 1.656 in the U.S. and 1.725 in Hong Kong.
- **Ablation Studies**: U.S. ablations showed significant reductions in IC and RankIC (after Holm adjustment) when major components were removed, validating the contribution of each module.

## 4. Conclusion
RICE-Alpha demonstrates that historical event continuation adds incremental information to stock forecasting when it is temporally grounded, reliability-calibrated, and introduced as a residual correction to a multi-view forecast. By decoupling base predictions from reliability-calibrated residual corrections, the method effectively addresses the limitations of existing LLM financial agents in handling temporal dependencies and event continuity.
