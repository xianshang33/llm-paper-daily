## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: On-policy distillation (OPD) is a promising paradigm for training language agents in multi-turn interactions, providing dense token-level teacher supervision on student-generated trajectories.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: Existing multi-turn OPD methods struggle with error accumulation. A single incorrect action alters the environment state, causing the student to diverge further from the teacher in subsequent turns. Preliminary experiments reveal that over half of failed rollouts contain an early "pivotal mistake"—an action that moves the agent away from task completion. Standard OPD fails to repair these mistakes because students rarely sample the correct recovery behaviors from these erroneous states, leading to persistent failures.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed PivotOPD**: An on-policy distillation framework that jointly trains the student to prevent pivotal mistakes and recover from the states they create.
- **Challenge 1: How to prevent pivotal mistakes?**
    - **Difficulty**: Pivotal mistakes often occur early, shifting the state distribution. Standard distillation struggles to correct this initial deviation as the student reinforces its own erroneous path.
    - **Solution**: Introduces **Preventive Distillation**. At the point of a pivotal mistake, the teacher provides the gold action. Reverse KL divergence is used to steer the student's probability distribution away from the pivotal mistake, teaching it to avoid such errors.
- **Challenge 2: How to recover from states caused by pivotal mistakes?**
    - **Difficulty**: Once in an erroneous state, the correct recovery path is a low-probability event in the student's exploration, making it difficult for standard forward KL distillation to learn effective recovery strategies.
    - **Solution**: Introduces **Recovery Distillation**. The teacher provides recovery actions for the few turns following the pivotal mistake. Forward KL divergence is used to transfer these rare recovery behaviors to the student, teaching it how to return to a successful trajectory from error states.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Setup**: Evaluated on ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search-based QA benchmarks using Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-8B student models, compared against 13 baselines.
- **Results**:
    - PivotOPD achieved the strongest average performance for both student models.
    - On ALFWorld, the 1.7B student improved by +5.5% over the strongest baseline.
    - Demonstrated strong generalization: In the software engineering domain (SWE-Bench Verified), it increased the resolve rate of a Nemotron-3.5 student by +3.2%.
- **Comparison**: Compared to standard OPD and other baselines, PivotOPD significantly reduced failures caused by early pivotal mistakes, proving its effectiveness in both error avoidance and recovery learning.

#### Summary
PivotOPD addresses the issue of error accumulation in multi-turn agent training caused by early pivotal mistakes. By combining Preventive Distillation (using reverse KL to avoid errors) and Recovery Distillation (using forward KL to learn recovery paths), the method teaches agents not only to make fewer mistakes but also how to self-correct when errors occur. Experiments show that PivotOPD outperforms state-of-the-art methods across multiple complex interaction benchmarks and exhibits generalization across different model families.
