## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: GPU kernel efficiency is critical for ML system performance. LLM agents have emerged as a promising tool for automating GPU kernel development through iterative code generation and optimization.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Current evaluations rely solely on runtime metrics, which fail to distinguish whether performance gaps stem from poor design discovery or flawed code implementation. This lack of diagnostic granularity hinders targeted improvements in agent capabilities.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed D2K-Bench**: A diagnostic benchmark comprising 26 tasks and 85 workloads to measure how effectively LLM agents translate expert design guidance into efficient GPU kernels.
- **Challenge 1: Quantifying the impact of expert design guidance.**
  - **Difficulty**: Runtime data alone cannot decouple the contributions of design discovery versus code implementation, making it hard to assess if agents truly understand and apply optimizations.
  - **Solution**: Introduced hierarchical expert guidance (L1: Algorithmic insights, L2: Dataflow design, L3: Low-level tricks) and conducted pairwise experiments with/without guidance under identical conditions to isolate the performance gains attributable to expert input.
- **Challenge 2: Diagnosing the gap between design and implementation.**
  - **Difficulty**: Agents may identify optimal designs but fail to implement them correctly due to resource management errors, a nuance missed by traditional benchmarks.
  - **Solution**: Complemented runtime assessments with evaluations of independently proposed designs and the actual implementation of design properties in the generated code, pinpointing specific failures in either exploration or engineering.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: Evaluated 5 frontier LLMs (including GPT-6-Astra, Claude-Opus-4.8, GPT-5.6-Sol) on NVIDIA B200 GPUs with a 350-turn interaction budget.
- **Results**:
  - **Correctness**: Expert guidance increased correctness across 130 model-task pairs from 93.1% to 98.5%.
  - **Performance Score**: Average performance score across all 26 tasks rose from 1.46 to 1.95.
  - **Speedup**: For the three top models achieving 100% correctness, geometric mean speedup improved from 1.69x to 2.49x.
  - **Implementation Score**: Mean combined implementation score increased from 57 to 70 out of 100.
- **Comparison**: The results demonstrate that while LLM agents are capable, expert guidance significantly bridges the gap in algorithmic and low-level optimization. The benchmark also highlights specific design properties that remain unimplemented, offering a clearer roadmap for improvement than previous runtime-only metrics.

## 4. Conclusion
D2K-Bench addresses the limitations of traditional benchmarks by introducing hierarchical expert guidance and multi-dimensional diagnostics. It proves the significant value of expert design input in enhancing the correctness and efficiency of LLM-generated GPU kernels. The study identifies specific bottlenecks in both design exploration and code implementation, providing actionable insights for advancing automated kernel development tools.
