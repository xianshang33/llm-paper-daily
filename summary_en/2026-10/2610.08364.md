## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Frontier AI evaluations are shifting from static benchmarks to open-ended, agentic, long-horizon tasks. These involve complex multi-agent networks producing transcripts spanning hundreds of pages and millions to billions of tokens.
- **Why Existing Work Fails**: As transcript length and complexity grow, the "observability envelope" (the range of behaviors evaluators can reliably infer) shrinks. While LLM assistants can help interpret behavior, they introduce significant analytical degrees of freedom, threatening the reproducibility and auditability of analyses. Static scores or simple summaries fail to capture intermediate processes, failure modes, and behavioral propensities in long-running tasks.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Transect**: An open-source package built on Inspect Scout designed to help evaluators understand how long agent runs unfold, identify behaviors worth investigating, and verify interpretations against the raw transcript.
- **Challenge 1: Unobservability and Complexity of Long Transcripts**. It is difficult for humans to parse narrative arcs and key events from massive token streams. **Solution**: Transect generates navigable reports that align recorded events, token usage, sub-agent activity, and model-generated behavioral labels on a common turn-based timeline, allowing reviewers to quickly grasp the run's narrative.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of Reproducibility and Auditability in LLM-assisted Analysis**. LLM-based analysis can vary significantly with settings, making conclusions hard to trace or reproduce. **Solution**: Transect separates task context/behavioral vocabulary (in reusable configs) from judge models/analysis settings. It allows tracing any label or event back to source turns and exporting underlying data tables for cross-run analysis, ensuring scientific rigor.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: Transect is an open-source package extending Inspect Scout, featuring a modular design that supports custom evaluation configurations, judge models, and analysis parameters.
- **Evaluation Results**: The workflow was demonstrated on an AI R&D evaluation generating nearly 13 million tokens. Transect successfully segmented agent work into behavioral phases aligned with research skill classifications, sub-agent delegations, and token usage.
- **Insights & Comparison**: The combined view revealed a focus on operational work and manuscript production, with little evidence of sustained hypothesis generation (arguably crucial for high-quality science). Unlike traditional methods focusing only on final scores, Transect provides deeper behavioral insights, uncovering process defects that might otherwise be missed.

## 4. Conclusion
Transect addresses the observability crisis in long-horizon, complex AI evaluations by providing a flexible, customizable transcript-analysis pipeline. It not only enhances evaluation efficiency, keeping pace with frequent and complex AI system iterations, but also supports scientific rigor in AI safety assessments by improving transparency, traceability, and reproducibility.
