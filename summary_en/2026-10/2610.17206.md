## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Modern supply chains operate in Volatile, Uncertain, Complex, and Ambiguous (VUCA) environments, facing diverse disruption risks such as natural hazards, environmental issues, transportation accidents, and socioeconomic vulnerabilities. Effective risk assessment is critical for maintaining supply chain continuity and operational resilience.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Existing Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) often lack fully integrated end-to-end automation, requiring partial human intervention and manual coordination, which hinders seamless execution. Furthermore, scalability is a major challenge in large-scale scenarios due to significant computational overhead caused by sequential interactions with data sources and analytical agents.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed AutoMARS Framework**: An agentic framework for automatic and scalable comparative risk assessment in supply chain networks.
- **Challenge 1: Lack of End-to-End Automation**. Existing methods rely on manual coordination, preventing seamless execution. AutoMARS addresses this by automatically planning tasks, coordinating specialized agents, invoking external analytical tools, and synthesizing domain-specific results into integrated risk evaluations, enabling fully automated end-to-end workflows.
- **Challenge 2: Poor Scalability in Large-Scale Assessments**. Traditional sequential processing leads to high computational latency. AutoMARS solves this by parallelizing independent analytical tasks, supporting scalable risk comparison across multiple locations and significantly reducing computational bottlenecks in large supply chain networks.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: AutoMARS adopts a multi-domain, multi-agent, and multi-tool architecture that integrates heterogeneous risk factors from socioeconomic, disaster, environmental, and transportation domains.
- **Evaluation Results**: The framework enables structured, systematic, and interpretable comparative analysis. It demonstrates improved efficiency and accuracy by automating tool invocation and leveraging parallel processing to handle large-scale data.
- **Comparison with Related Work**: Compared to previous frameworks like MARS and traditional optimization or simulation methods, AutoMARS eliminates the need for human intervention and introduces parallel processing mechanisms, offering superior automation levels and scalability for handling large numbers of supply chain nodes.

## 4. Conclusion
AutoMARS is an innovative multi-agent LLM framework addressing the pain points of automation and scalability in supply chain risk assessment. By integrating heterogeneous data sources, automating workflow planning, and employing parallel task processing, it achieves comprehensive, efficient, and interpretable risk evaluation, providing robust decision support for supply chain location selection and risk management.
