## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: LLM agents are increasingly capable of gathering and synthesizing evidence from single homogeneous sources (structured tables or unstructured text). However, real-world analytical questions often span heterogeneous sources, requiring both database computations and web-based evidence.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: While benchmarks like HybridDeepResearch have exposed the struggle of frontier agents in crossing the structured-unstructured boundary, there is a lack of methodologies to explicitly teach cross-source reasoning. Current training recipes typically operate within single sources, failing to address the complexity of hybrid reasoning chains.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed HybridResearcher**: A 4B parameter agent specifically trained to bridge evidence across the structured and unstructured boundary.
- **Challenge 1: How to construct high-quality cross-source reasoning training data?**
  - **Difficulty**: Real-world labeled data combining structured and unstructured dependencies is scarce, and it is hard to ensure true interdependence rather than simple juxtaposition.
  - **Solution**: The paper synthesizes cross-source bridge trajectories by starting with validated single-source tasks (e.g., Text-to-SQL or web search) and fabricating the missing source around a "bridge entity" from the seed output. Strict construction gates ensure that neither side alone suffices, guaranteeing true cross-source interdependence.
- **Challenge 2: How to teach complex cross-source reasoning to small models without catastrophic forgetting?**
  - **Difficulty**: Training small models on complex cross-source tasks risks degrading their performance on single-source tasks (catastrophic forgetting).
  - **Solution**: The model is trained using Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) on the curated dataset. This approach improves cross-source reasoning while also outperforming the base model on held-out single-source benchmarks (BrowseComp and BrowseComp-Plus), demonstrating effective retention of general capabilities.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: Built on the Qwen3.5-4B base model, trained with synthesized cross-source trajectories via SFT and RL.
- **Evaluation Results**:
  - On the HybridDeepResearch benchmark, HybridResearcher-4B improves over its base model (Qwen3.5-4B) by 30.0 points in pass@8.
  - It surpasses the much larger Qwen3.5-397B-A17B model by 8.3 points.
  - It also improves over the base model on single-source benchmarks, indicating no catastrophic forgetting.
- **Comparison**: Significantly outperforms both the larger counterpart and the untrained base small model, proving the efficacy of specialized cross-source training for compact models.

## 4. Conclusion
HybridResearcher successfully addresses the challenge of cross-source reasoning between structured and unstructured data through innovative data synthesis and a two-stage training strategy. It achieves superior reasoning performance on small-parameter models compared to much larger models, while maintaining competitiveness in single-source tasks, offering a new paradigm for efficient and specialized deep research agents.
