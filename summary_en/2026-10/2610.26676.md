## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: LLM agentic workflows increasingly involve multiple specialized roles (e.g., planning, solving). Using the most capable models for all roles is prohibitively expensive, necessitating a balance between task quality, deployment cost, and latency.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current methods like AgentOpt reduce multi-objective search to single-objective optimization with fixed weights, while others like SCOPE optimize under strict constraints. These approaches lack flexibility in recommending complete role assignments without pre-specified trade-offs and suffer from the high computational cost of exhaustive evaluation due to the multiplicative growth of candidate assignments.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Cost-Coupled Gittins (CC-Gittins)**: A multi-objective extension of cost-aware Gittins search designed for Pareto recommendation.
- **Challenge 1: High Search Cost due to Coupled Evaluation and Deployment Costs**. The difficulty lies in the exponential number of candidates and the expense of evaluating each workflow end-to-end. CC-Gittins exploits the natural coupling where higher deployment costs imply higher evaluation costs. It alternates between seeking higher quality and lower deployment cost, reusing each evaluation to inform both searches, thereby significantly reducing total search overhead.
- **Challenge 2: Recommending Pareto Frontiers under Uncertainty**. Identifying non-dominated solutions with limited evaluations is challenging. CC-Gittins recommends assignments that are nondominated based on uncertainty-adjusted estimates of quality and cost after each batch, providing anytime recommendations without requiring fixed preferences or final search budgets.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Setup**: Evaluated on eight quality-cost matrices from workflows with 2-5 roles and up to 1,681 candidate assignments, including a third objective (latency).
- **Results**: CC-Gittins recovers a substantial fraction of the true Pareto frontier at roughly 10% of the exhaustive evaluation cost. By 30% of the cost, it recovers most of the frontier with few false positives.
- **Comparison**: Outperforms exhaustive search in efficiency. For instance, on the BIRD Dev benchmark, it achieved 88.75% mean recall and 100% mean precision at 90% lower evaluation cost than exhaustive methods, demonstrating superior cost-efficiency compared to static or single-objective baselines.

#### Summary
This paper addresses the model assignment problem in LLM agentic workflows by introducing CC-Gittins. By leveraging the coupling between evaluation and deployment costs, the algorithm efficiently identifies Pareto-optimal trade-offs between quality, cost, and latency. It significantly reduces the computational burden of search while providing flexible, high-quality recommendations without predefined user preferences.
