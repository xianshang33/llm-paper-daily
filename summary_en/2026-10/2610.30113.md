## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Quantitative research is an iterative loop involving hypothesis proposal, implementation as executable signals, historical testing, and strategy selection. Large Language Models (LLMs) are naturally suited for this loop as they can propose hypotheses, write code, interpret feedback, and revise approaches.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Existing benchmarks (e.g., AlphaQT-Bench, AlphaBench, StockBench) only evaluate code correctness, formulaic factor generation, or individual trading decisions. They fail to assess whether LLMs can complete the full end-to-end research loop on real market data and produce strategies that generalize beyond the research period (out-of-sample performance).

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed Quant-Arena**: A benchmark and public leaderboard for end-to-end quantitative research on real A-share market data, evaluating the complete workflow from feature construction to strategy selection.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in End-to-End Strategy Generation and Evaluation in Real Markets**. It is challenging to ensure strategies are not only code-correct but also profitable after accounting for trading costs and market impact, while avoiding overfitting. The paper addresses this by allowing models to iterate only on 2022-2023 data with limited feedback, then freezing the strategy for out-of-sample assessment on sealed 2024 data, using a simulated order book to estimate market impact.
- **Challenge 2: Difficulty in Comprehensive Multi-dimensional Strategy Assessment**. Relying solely on backtest returns is insufficient; signal quality and feature diversity must also be considered. The paper introduces a scoring system dominated by realized excess return, complemented by signal quality and feature diversity metrics, providing a more holistic view of strategy quality.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: Models access raw Level-2 data (orders, trades, order books) via a controlled interface. They construct features and scoring code, iterate within a fixed budget on 2022-2023 data, and submit one frozen strategy. The evaluator executes this strategy on 2024 data, applying lot-size constraints and order-dependent costs including market impact.
- **Results**: Nine frontier models were evaluated. GPT-6 Astra achieved the highest score. However, no model beat the equal-weight market benchmark in 2024.
- **Comparison**: Unlike AlphaQT-Bench (code only), AlphaBench (factors only), or StockBench (sequential decisions), Quant-Arena is the first to evaluate the joint effect of feature construction, signal revision, and strategy selection on real market data with strict out-of-sample testing.

## 4. Conclusion
Quant-Arena fills the gap in evaluating LLMs for end-to-end quantitative research. By enforcing strict out-of-sample testing and realistic trading cost simulations, it reveals that current state-of-the-art LLM agents cannot yet consistently beat the market benchmark in real A-share trading. The benchmark is open for submissions, providing a crucial standard for future advancements in LLM-driven financial quantification.
