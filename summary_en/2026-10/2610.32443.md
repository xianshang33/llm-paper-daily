## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Pre-consultation medical dialogues are crucial for gathering comprehensive patient information for triage and diagnosis. However, acquiring large-scale real-world doctor-patient dialogues is challenging due to privacy regulations, ethical constraints, and expensive expert annotation.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Existing LLM-based synthesis methods often rely on prompt engineering or rigid rules. These approaches struggle to balance clinical relevance with realistic patient diversity. Prompt-driven methods suffer from hallucinations and limited clinical reliability, while rule-based or iterative multi-agent methods face a trade-off between scalability and reliability, often requiring costly manual verification or complex pipelines.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed CoRPS-Dial**: A controllable dual-agent framework for pre-consultation medical dialogue synthesis that integrates patient personas with guideline-aligned clinical workflow rules.
- **Challenge 1: Balancing Clinical Standardization with Patient Realism.**
  - **Difficulty**: Traditional single-agent generation or rigid rules fail to simulate diverse patient expressions while maintaining strict diagnostic logic.
  - **Solution**: CoRPS-Dial decouples patient personas and clinical workflows by using a "persona-driven patient agent" for realistic behavior and a "process-guided doctor agent" to ensure adherence to clinical guidelines during interaction.
- **Challenge 2: Ensuring Global Consistency and Quality in Long Dialogues.**
  - **Difficulty**: Multi-turn dialogues often suffer from role inconsistency, missing workflow steps, or logical breaks. Real-time validation is computationally expensive and prone to interruption.
  - **Solution**: The framework employs a post-hoc quality control mechanism that scores role consistency, workflow completeness, and textual consistency, followed by selective refinement of low-scoring samples, improving efficiency and quality.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Experimental Setup**: Experiments were conducted in the domain of coronary heart disease to evaluate the alignment between synthetic and real dialogues.
- **Metrics**: Information coverage, clinical compliance, and dialogue naturalness.
- **Results & Comparison**:
  - CoRPS-Dial outperforms existing methods (including prompt-based, intent-graph, and rigid rule-based approaches) across all evaluation metrics.
  - Downstream fine-tuning experiments confirm that synthetic data generated by CoRPS-Dial significantly improves performance on medical dialogue tasks.
  - Compared to iterative methods requiring real-time rule validation and repeated LLM calls, CoRPS-Dial's post-hoc validation and selective revision strategy reduce computational complexity while maintaining high quality.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper presents CoRPS-Dial, a dual-agent framework that decouples patient personas from clinical workflows for medical dialogue generation. By separating the simulation of patient diversity from the enforcement of clinical protocols, and incorporating efficient post-hoc quality control, the method effectively addresses the trade-off between clinical reliability and data diversity. Experiments demonstrate that the generated data closely aligns with real dialogues and enhances downstream medical AI models, offering a scalable solution for acquiring high-quality medical dialogue data.
