## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: Games have long served as AI milestones (e.g., Go, StarCraft), traditionally built by large teams of human experts. With LLM agents now capable of writing code, research is shifting towards whether agents can automate the entire research process.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Current evidence of LLM agent capabilities is limited to benchmark tasks or paper reviews. There is no proof that agents can handle the full pipeline from raw data to a competitive system in complex, real-world problems like 'Hearthstone Battlegrounds,' which features stochasticity, hidden information, and nearly 1,000 interacting cards. Previous AI efforts lacked faithful simulators of the live game, serving only as advisors or studying simplified versions.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed AutoTavern**: A self-improving research system where specialized LLM research agents share typed research memory, pass claims through verification gates, and close the loop with live games to iteratively improve the simulator, opponents, policy, and their own research process.
- **Challenge 1: Accurate Modeling of Complex Game Environments**. Creating a high-fidelity simulator is difficult due to random shops, battles, hidden info, and massive card interactions. AutoTavern solved this by having agents build 'TavernGym' in 20 days, the first faithful, full-content reproduction of the live game, aided by human-defined rules for ambiguous mechanics.
- **Challenge 2: Achieving Super-Human Decision-Making Under Strict Real-Time Constraints**. Real-time turns require decisions in <1 second, whereas LLMs are slow and inaccurate. The paper addressed this by using agents to generate 'TavernAgent,' trained with PPO against calibrated opponents. This resulted in decision times of 0.03 seconds and accuracy far surpassing frontier LLMs, demonstrating that 'LLM agents build far better than they play.'

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: AutoTavern built TavernGym and produced 23 versions of TavernAgent. TavernAgent plays complete games independently on the live ladder.
- **Evaluation Results**:
    - **Ladder Performance**: TavernAgent's rating rose from 6,000 to ~15,000. In its last 20 games, it achieved an average placement of 1.60 with an 80% first-place rate, outperforming the server's top 10 human players (avg placement 2.34, 59% first-place rate).
    - **TavernBench Benchmark**: Across 960 tasks in 40 scenarios, TavernAgent solved 91% of tasks, compared to a maximum of 63% for frontier LLMs (e.g., GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5).
    - **Speed Comparison**: TavernAgent decides in 0.03 seconds, while frontier LLMs take up to 26 seconds. Within the 1-second limit of a live turn, TavernAgent maintains 90% performance, whereas other LLMs fail due to latency.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper demonstrates that LLM agents can independently build complex real-world systems from raw data that surpass top human performance. Through AutoTavern, the authors developed a high-fidelity simulator and a super-human AI agent for Hearthstone Battlegrounds. The key insight is that 'LLM agents are better at building systems than playing games,' as the specialized AI systems constructed by agents significantly outperform direct LLM gameplay in both efficiency and accuracy. Code and benchmarks are open-sourced.
