## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Statistical theory papers rely on long proofs that are rarely checked line-by-line by readers or referees, allowing errors in routine steps to go unnoticed until corrections are published. Formalizing these proofs in assistants like Lean 4 is reliable but requires weeks of expert effort, while using Large Language Models (LLMs) alone produces informal arguments that are hard to verify automatically and prone to hallucinations.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Expert-led formalization is too costly and slow for widespread use. Pure LLM-based checking lacks rigorous verification, often leading to false confidence or proving statements that differ subtly from the original text without detection.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed KAPPA (Kit of Agents for Proof-checking Papers with a proof Assistant)**: An open-source software kit that orchestrates LLM agents (run via Claude Code) to translate LaTeX proofs into Lean 4 code with Mathlib, verifying them step-by-step against the proof assistant's kernel.
- **Challenge 1: Ensuring consistency between formalized proofs and original statements**. The difficulty lies in LLMs potentially altering proposition conditions, leading to the verification of a different (often narrower) theorem than intended. Solution: The system compares each statement with the paper before proving and fingerprints it, ensuring no drift occurs, and bases every verdict on reproducible Lean evidence.
- **Challenge 2: Handling complex dependencies and error localization in long proofs**. Statistical proofs mix cited results with many routine steps, making errors hard to pinpoint. Solution: KAPPA uses separate writing and reviewing agents that follow the paper's original proof structure rather than finding new proofs. It assigns one of four verdicts (verified unconditionally, verified conditionally, issue recorded, unfinished) and precisely identifies failing steps and responsible parties.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: KAPPA runs unattended for hours, handles API rate limits, re-checks only changed parts upon revision, and reports costs by role, model, and result. It ensures transparency by providing evidence for every verdict.
- **Evaluation Results**:
    - Tested on four example papers with answer keys and three published statistical papers (blind-tested using versions prior to public corrections).
    - In all three published papers, KAPPA successfully identified the published errors at the exact steps named in the corrections, along with additional issues confirmed manually.
- **Comparison with Related Work**:
    - Compared to a baseline where the same LLM works directly with Lean without the KAPPA agent architecture, KAPPA correctly classified more results on the longest paper.
    - The baseline method reported some statements narrower than the paper's as "proved," risking overclaiming. In contrast, no verdict from KAPPA claimed more than its Lean evidence supported, ensuring rigorous accuracy.

## 4. Conclusion
KAPPA effectively combines the translation capabilities of LLMs with the rigorous formal verification of Lean 4, offering an efficient, automated solution for checking statistical paper proofs. It addresses the high cost of traditional formalization and the unreliability of pure LLM checking, accurately detecting and localizing errors while ensuring transparency and reproducibility, thereby significantly enhancing the reliability of statistical theory research.
