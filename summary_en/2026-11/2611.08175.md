## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Automating Printed Circuit Board (PCB) routing requires coordinating board-level connectivity decisions with geometric execution under strict design rules (e.g., clearance, trace width, vias). It is a coupled physical design problem where each decision impacts subsequent connections.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Traditional rule-based methods rely on heuristic searches and struggle with complex constraints. While recent agents can interact with native routing tools, reliable routing on complex boards remains challenging due to the lack of coordination between global strategy and local execution, akin to human engineering workflows.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed**: PCBRouteAgent, a multimodal PCB routing agent inspired by how human engineers coordinate global strategy with local execution.
- **Challenge 1: Disconnection between global topology planning and local geometric execution**. The difficulty lies in balancing board-level electrical topology, routing order, and layer transitions with strict geometric constraints. The paper addresses this by using a "Global Routing Planner" that combines board images and structured electrical/geometric information to specify connection topology, corridors, and layer transitions.
- **Challenge 2: Efficient error recovery and iterative optimization**. The challenge is correcting local execution failures without disrupting accepted routes. The paper solves this via a "Detailed Routing Executor" that uses KiCad's native engine for bounded local recovery and returns unresolved failures as structured feedback, enabling targeted guide revisions while preserving successful paths.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: PCBRouteAgent consists of a Global Routing Planner and a Detailed Routing Executor. It was evaluated on 247 synthetic and real-world boards across four splits of the PCBWorld-Bench.
- **Evaluation Results**: The agent achieved clean-pass rates of 100%, 94.9%, 100%, and 40% on the D2, D3-A, D3-B, and D3-C datasets, respectively.
- **Comparison**: On the three real-world subsets, PCBRouteAgent outperformed the best-evaluated baseline in clean-pass rate by 13.9, 36, and 10 percentage points, respectively, demonstrating significant superiority over existing methods.

#### Summary
PCBRouteAgent effectively addresses the coordination challenges in complex PCB routing by mimicking the collaborative mechanism of human engineers' global planning and local execution. Its core innovation lies in fusing multimodal information for global planning and employing a structured feedback mechanism for efficient local error recovery. Experimental results show that the method significantly outperforms current state-of-the-art baselines in real-world scenarios, highlighting the potential of LLM agents to approach human-engineer levels in specialized engineering design tasks.
