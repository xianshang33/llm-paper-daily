## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: With the rise of personal AI agents, decentralized Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) are becoming prevalent. However, existing MAS are predominantly centralized, relying on a global orchestrator for managing agents, resources, and communication.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Centralized architectures face three major issues: 1) **Scalability and Maintenance**: Registering all knowledge and resources centrally becomes prohibitively expensive at web scale; 2) **Privacy Concerns**: Skills dependent on private data or specialized runtimes cannot be shared in a central repository; 3) **Lack of Proactive Collaboration**: Without a global orchestrator, existing methods struggle to enable agents to proactively discover non-local resources and collaborate with neighbors.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed DecArena benchmark and GraphHarness baseline**.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in discovering non-local resources in decentralized environments**. Agents only observe local states and interact with neighbors, making it hard to find relevant resources among 100K+ distributed tools. **Solution**: DecArena establishes a benchmark for synthetic scalable distributed tool calling under graph-constrained communication, exposing the failures of current LLM agents in this setting.
- **Challenge 2: Failure of standard Reinforcement Learning (RL) in decentralized collaboration**. Experiments show that standard RL cannot reliably overcome collaboration limitations even with SOTA LLMs due to the lack of explicit coordination mechanisms. **Solution**: GraphHarness is introduced, combining topology-aware warmup with self-improving LLM-based routing to explicitly harness network structures for effective collaboration.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Benchmark**: DecArena features synthetic distributed tool calling tasks with over 100,000 tools under graph-constrained communication.
- **Key Findings**: Recent LLM agents struggle with non-local resource discovery and collaboration in decentralized settings; standard RL fails to reliably address these limitations.
- **GraphHarness Performance**: Compared to baselines, GraphHarness substantially improves task success rate (+48.5%), produces higher quality collaboration (+30.6%), and achieves better cost efficiency (+34.0% reduction in cost).

#### Summary
This paper addresses scalability, privacy, and proactive collaboration challenges in decentralized MAS by introducing the DecArena benchmark. It reveals the limitations of existing LLM and RL methods under graph-constrained communication. The proposed GraphHarness method, utilizing topology-aware warmup and self-improving routing, effectively solves non-local resource discovery and collaboration issues, outperforming baselines in success rate, collaboration quality, and cost efficiency.
