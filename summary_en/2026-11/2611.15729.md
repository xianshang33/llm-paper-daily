## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Enterprise agents accumulate memory across dozens of sessions, facing contradictory facts, superseded information, and confusion among similarly named entities in embedding space.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current research treats this as a retrieval problem, but experiments show that recall metrics (Retention and Currency) are nearly saturated (0.93-0.98). The remaining bottleneck is "Resolution": identifying the correct entity, determining the current time-state, and deciding whether to abstain when evidence is insufficient. Traditional methods lack effective disambiguation mechanisms and terminal state contracts, leading to hallucinations or forced answers.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed DISCERN**, an agent-memory framework based on the principle: "Preserve at Write, Resolve at Read."
- **Challenge 1: Precise Entity and Temporal Resolution**. It is difficult to accurately disambiguate entities and time states with a single post-retrieval LLM call due to collisions and updates. DISCERN addresses this by building a strictly additive bi-temporal provenance graph at write time (never merging confusable entities or dropping superseded values) and using an agentic loop driven by a zero-LLM tool surface at read time to navigate this graph.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of Abstention Mechanism**. Existing systems often force an answer even when unanswerable. DISCERN introduces four gated terminal actions (ANSWER/UPDATE/CLARIFY/ABSTAIN) during the read phase, allowing the system to correctly choose "Abstain" when evidence is insufficient, significantly improving calibration.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Benchmarks**: ENT-310 (synthetic enterprise sales-agent memory benchmark with 210 answerable and 100 unanswerable queries) and the full LoCoMo corpus.
- **Results**:
    - On ENT-310, DISCERN achieved an F1 score of 0.924 for abstention decisions, significantly outperforming the best baseline of 0.802.
    - In macro answer correctness, DISCERN scored 0.861, surpassing the strongest baseline of 0.806. This lead was driven by entity resolution capabilities (0.800 for DISCERN vs. <0.40 for all baselines).
    - When applied with zero tuning to the LoCoMo dataset, DISCERN again led in macro answer correctness (0.663 vs. 0.638) and remained among the best-calibrated systems for false-premise abstention.

## 4. Conclusion
DISCERN demonstrates that the core challenge of persistent agent memory has shifted from retrieval to resolution. By constructing a bi-temporal provenance graph and introducing an agentic read-resolution mechanism, DISCERN effectively solves entity disambiguation and abstention under uncertainty. It significantly outperforms existing RAG and memory systems across multiple benchmarks, providing a more reliable and better-calibrated memory solution for enterprise agents.
