## 1. What the Paper Did
- **Background**: The release of models like GPT-6 Astra marks the emergence of "Agentic Robotics." Unlike traditional methods that train dedicated policies for specific tasks, agentic robotic systems use foundation models for open-world embodied tasks, operating in a closed loop of observation, reasoning, action, and verification.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: The community lacks a shared definition, reliable evaluation framework, or research roadmap. Digital agent methods fail to directly transfer to the physical world due to constraints such as limited reaction time, mechanical limits, unpredictable environments, and irreversible consequences, making it difficult to translate general-purpose reasoning into effective physical actions.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed an "Embodied System 2" framework and evaluation system**: Argues that a foundation model combined with an "embodied harness" forms the decision-making core, and defines a four-level autonomy roadmap from executing primitives to fulfilling long-term roles.
- **Challenge 1: Difficulty in translating reasoning to action under physical constraints**. Physical realities (real-time requirements, irreversibility) make direct digital-to-physical translation unsafe. The paper addresses this by proposing an "embodied harness" that maps general reasoning to skills verified against physical constraints, ensuring action validity.
- **Challenge 2: Lack of unified evaluation standards and research paths**. Current benchmarks ignore closed-loop system performance and long-term reliability. The paper proposes a new evaluation framework measuring both model capabilities and closed-loop performance, alongside a research roadmap from L1 (primitives) to L4 (sustained roles) to guide sustainable physical autonomy.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation & Deployment**: As a vision paper, it does not provide specific code or single-hardware deployment details but offers a reference architecture and theoretical principles. It emphasizes the integration of foundation models with an embodied harness and the transfer of principles from digital to physical agents.
- **Evaluation & Comparison**: The paper notes that frontier models with agentic harnesses already match or exceed task-specific policies on many benchmarks. However, it critiques current evaluations for ignoring physical constraints. The proposed framework aims to fill this gap by assessing safety, reliability, and responsibility over longer time horizons (L2-L4 levels), contrasting with traditional single-task success metrics.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper envisions a new era of Agentic Robotics, establishing "Embodied System 2" as the decision-making core. By distinguishing between digital and physical agents, it identifies the core challenge of translating reasoning into physical action under constraints. It provides a corresponding evaluation framework and a four-level development roadmap, pointing the way toward safe, reliable, and broadly responsible sustained physical autonomy.
