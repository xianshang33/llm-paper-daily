## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Reinforcement learning (RL) via environmental interaction is a cornerstone post-training paradigm for evolving Large Language Models (LLMs) into autonomous agents capable of multi-turn tool use and exploration. The structure of the environment directly bounds the capabilities an agent can acquire.
- **Limitations of Existing Work**: Current structured and retrieval environments rely heavily on upstream pre-filtering, such as extracting compact subgraphs around gold answers or retrieving fixed passages before the policy acts. This introduces a fundamental measurement confound: by enclosing ground-truth paths, these subgraphs compress the candidate space by orders of magnitude and leak answer locations via local topology. Consequently, even question-blind heuristics can surface correct answers far above chance, meaning path selection and pruning are not genuine policy decisions.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed RISE (Interactive Structured Environment)**: A framework that transitions structured RL from static in-context subgraphs to full-scale interactive databases.
- **Challenge 1: Constrained Decision Space and Answer Leakage**. Pre-filtering artificially reduces search complexity, allowing agents to guess answers without true reasoning. **Solution**: Introduces "Decision-Space Scaling," which exposes the unpruned knowledge graph via a five-stage graph-agnostic typed intent adapter under a bounded call budget and a multiplicative grounding reward gate, forcing genuine decision-making.
- **Challenge 2: Within-Group Variance Collapse**. Full-graph exploration under group-normalized objectives suffers from severe variance collapse, hindering effective learning. **Solution**: Proposes "Learning-Frontier Scaling," which unites model-independent structural tier progression with a self-evolving competence-frontier bandit to overcome variance collapse and stabilize training.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Experimental Setup**: Evaluated across 19 benchmarks and three model backbones.
- **Key Results**:
    - **In-Domain & Unseen Schema**: RISE substantially advances performance in knowledge-graph reasoning for both in-domain and unseen schemas.
    - **Zero-Shot Transfer**: Demonstrates strong zero-shot transfer capabilities to open-domain passage retrieval.
    - **Out-of-Domain Enhancement**: Significantly enhances out-of-domain mathematical and logical deduction skills.
- **Comparison**: Unlike existing methods that suffer from inflated performance due to environmental biases (pre-filtering), RISE restores path selection and candidate pruning as authentic policy decisions. It demonstrates superior generalization and true reasoning capabilities by training in a complete, interactive environment rather than static, biased subgraphs.

## 4. Conclusion
This paper identifies the critical issue of evaluation bias in LLM agent RL environments caused by pre-filtering. It proposes RISE, which addresses answer leakage and training variance collapse through Decision-Space Scaling and Learning-Frontier Scaling. By expanding the training environment to full-scale interactive databases, RISE not only improves knowledge graph reasoning but also enables zero-shot transfer to open-domain retrieval and enhances logical deduction, offering a new paradigm for developing truly autonomous and reasoning-capable LLM agents.
