## 1. What the paper did
- **Background**: Agentic AI has the potential to automate the analysis of sensitive clinical data, but current systems rely heavily on Large Language Models (LLMs).
- **Limitations of existing work**: Cloud-based LLMs pose privacy risks by exposing patient data. Local LLMs preserve privacy but suffer from hallucinations, where invented values in tool calls propagate errors through subsequent steps. Additionally, text-generation-based reasoning is inefficient in terms of time and energy.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed System 1 Agents framework**: A method for building local, reliable, and fast agentic AI that calls tools without generating text.
- **Challenge 1: Reliability and Hallucinations in Local Models**. The difficulty lies in traditional LLMs generating tool calls token-by-token, leading to fabricated arguments or non-existent tools. The solution involves a deterministic compiler that converts tool schemas and requests into multiple-choice questions, answered by a one-pass decision model, thereby eliminating text-generation hallucinations.
- **Challenge 2: Balancing Privacy and Efficiency**. The challenge is that cloud models leak privacy while local small models are less capable and slower due to complex reasoning. The solution adopts a "System 1" fast-judgment mechanism that performs discrete choices without chain-of-thought reasoning, ensuring data stays local while significantly improving speed and energy efficiency.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation**: The framework consists of three components: an Agent controlling the workflow, a Deterministic Compiler converting schemas/requests into multiple-choice questions, and a Decision Model answering them in a single forward pass. The code is open-source.
- **Evaluation Results**: Tested on 1,952 requests across 157 biomedical tools, the best System 1 agent achieved 97.0% accuracy in selecting the correct tool or correctly declining. It filled 97.0% of 6,056 stated argument values correctly.
- **Comparison with Related Work**: System 1 agents significantly outperformed all 18 local LLM-agent settings (best was 95.2%). Crucially, System 1 calls had 0% invented content, whereas LLM calls exhibited hallucination rates between 5.5% and 19.7%.

#### Summary
This paper introduces System 1 Agents, a novel approach that transforms tool calling into a multiple-choice decision problem, bypassing the hallucination issues inherent in text-generating LLMs. By ensuring data locality for privacy and achieving higher accuracy, reliability, and speed than existing local LLM agents, this method is particularly suited for clinical data analysis scenarios where privacy and correctness are paramount.
