## 1. What the Paper Does
- **Background**: Large Language Model (LLM) agents are typically improved via agentic reinforcement learning for interactive decision-making. However, existing post-training methods face limitations at test time.
- **Why Existing Work Falls Short**: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) is data-efficient but off-policy, suffering from distribution shift and difficulty in collecting golden trajectories for open-world tasks. Reinforcement Learning (RL) is on-policy but relies on sparse trajectory-level rewards and expensive rollouts, limiting its real-time application during testing.

## Core Contributions
- **Proposed a verbally Supervised Fine-Tuning (vSFT) framework**: This framework enables the combination of dense natural language feedback with weight updates at test time, allowing for agent self-evolution.
- **Challenge 1: How to effectively perform parameter updates using unstructured natural language feedback at test time?**
  - **Difficulty**: Traditional methods struggle to translate text feedback into specific model weight adjustments, and test-time backpropagation is computationally prohibitive.
  - **Solution**: Introduces a Hypernetwork that converts verbal supervision (skills and reflections) generated by a supervisor directly into Low-Rank Adapter (LoRA) weight updates, enabling parameter-in evolution without backpropagation.
- **Challenge 2: How to overcome the high cost of exploration and sparse rewards in Reinforcement Learning?**
  - **Difficulty**: RL requires extensive trial-and-error to receive sparse rewards, leading to slow convergence and high computational costs.
  - **Solution**: Establishes a closed loop between the agent and a supervisor. The supervisor extracts dense verbal feedback from failed trajectories, which the hypernetwork uses to progressively update the LoRA, allowing the agent to adapt quickly with fewer trials.

## Implementation and Evaluation
- **Implementation Details**: vSFT forms a closed loop where system prompts and few-shot examples are initialized into a LoRA. After each failed attempt, the supervisor generates verbal supervision, which the hypernetwork converts into new LoRA weights attached to the agent, all without backpropagation.
- **Evaluation Results**: vSFT based on Qwen3-4B significantly outperforms existing methods on ALFWorld and WebShop benchmarks.
- **Comparison with Related Work**:
  - On ALFWorld, vSFT achieves an 8.7% higher success rate than GRPO and 13.9% higher than Reflexion.
  - On WebShop, vSFT achieves a 4.8% higher success rate than GRPO and 18.2% higher than Reflexion.
  - Demonstrates that test-time parameter-in evolution outperforms both in-context learning counterparts and prevalent reinforcement learning methods.

## 4. Conclusion
vSFT proposes a new paradigm of "learning how to adapt a model" rather than just "using an adapted model." By converting natural language feedback into test-time low-rank weight updates via a hypernetwork, vSFT effectively addresses the high cost and sparse reward issues of traditional RL. It enables efficient self-evolution and performance improvement of agents during the testing phase, offering a promising path towards more capable autonomous agents.
